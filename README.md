# Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard – India

**Overview:-**

This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Amazon’s sales performance in India, showcasing revenue trends, profit distribution, customer behavior, and product performance. The goal of the project is to derive actionable business insights from raw sales data and present them through interactive visualizations that support data-driven decision-making.

**Objectives:-**
- Understand overall sales and profit performance across categories and regions.
- Identify top-performing products, customer segments, and states.
- Analyze seasonal trends and high-demand periods throughout the year.
- Monitor discounts, profit margins, and delivery performance to optimize sales strategy.

**Key Insights:-**
- Top Categories: Electronics and Fashion dominate total revenue and order volume.
- Regional Insights: Maharashtra, Delhi, and Karnataka consistently lead in sales.
- Sales Trends: Clear spikes in October–December, aligning with festive shopping seasons.
- Profitability: High-discount categories show lower margins; premium categories sustain better profitability.
- Customer Segment: Repeat purchases are higher among Prime/fast delivery customers.

**Dashboard Features:-**
- KPIs Displayed: Total Sales, Profit %, Order Count, Quantity Sold
- Interactive Filters: Product Category, State, Year, and Delivery Mode
- Visualizations Used:
    - Bar Charts – Category-wise and region-wise performance
    - Line Charts – Month-over-month trend analysis
    - Maps – Geographical sales distribution across India
    - Pie Charts – Order mode and segment-based contribution
- Dynamic Tooltips for precise values and percentage comparisons
- User-Friendly Layout for quick insights at both macro and micro levels

**Dataset Details:-**
The dataset contains key transactional and operational sales fields such as:
  - Order ID, Order Date, Ship Mode
  - Product Category and Sub-Category
  - Sales, Profit, Quantity
  - Customer Segment, City, and State

Data cleaning and preprocessing were performed using Excel before connecting to Tableau.

**Data Source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thedevastator/unlock-profits-with-e-commerce-sales-data?select=Amazon+Sale+Report.csv
